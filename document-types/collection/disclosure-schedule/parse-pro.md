# DISCLOSURE SCHEDULES

Reference is hereby made to that certain Asset Purchase Agreement (the “Agreement”),
dated as of July 20, 2026, by and among Finch Therapeutics Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation,
Finch Therapeutics, Inc., a Delaware corporation, Finch Therapeutics Holdings LLC, a Delaware
limited liability company, and Finch Research and Development LLC, a Delaware limited liability
company (each a “Seller” and collectively, the “Sellers”), and Ferring Asset Holding LLC, a
Delaware limited liability company (the “Purchaser” and, together with Sellers, the “Parties”).
Included herein are the schedules to the Agreement (collectively, the “Disclosure Schedules”).
Capitalized terms used herein and not otherwise defined shall have the meanings set forth in the
Agreement.


These Disclosure Schedules are qualified in their entirety by reference to the Agreement,
and are not intended to constitute, and shall not be construed as constituting, representations and
warranties of Sellers, except to the extent provided in the Agreement.


The schedules attached hereto form an integral part of the Disclosure Schedules.
Disclosure of any information in the Disclosure Schedules that is not strictly required under the
Agreement has been made for informational purposes only and does not imply disclosure of all
matters of a similar nature. The disclosure of any event, item or occurrence set forth on the
Disclosure Schedules shall apply to, qualify or modify the section or subsection of the Agreement
to which it corresponds and each of the other sections of the Agreement to the extent that its
relevance to such other section or subsection of the Agreement is reasonably apparent on its face.
The insertion of headings is for convenient reference only and does not affect the interpretation of
these Disclosure Schedules.
