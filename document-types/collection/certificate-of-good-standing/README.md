## Why this document

### Who processes it

A certificate of good standing is the state's confirmation that an entity exists and is
current with its filings. Counterparties ask for one before they do business with the
entity.

- **Commercial lending:** lenders require a recent certificate before closing a loan to
  a corporation or LLC.
- **Mergers, acquisitions and corporate law:** attorneys collect certificates for every
  entity in a transaction as closing deliverables.
- **Banking and KYB onboarding:** banks verify that a business is active when opening
  accounts.
- **Government contracting and grants:** agencies and funders confirm an applicant's
  status before an award.
- **Commercial leasing and licensing:** landlords and licensing boards check that the
  entity signing is in good standing.

### What it contains

- **Values embedded in prose.** Every value sits in a blank inside a sentence, not
  beside a label.
- **An out-of-order text layer.** The filled-in values are written to the PDF after the
  template, so they come at the end of the page text rather than in their sentences.
- **Several near-identical dates with different meanings:** a paper-filings-through
  date, an electronic-filings-through date and time, and the issue date and time.
- **A state seal** and a **facsimile signature**.
- **A validation notice** with a confirmation number and a URL.
