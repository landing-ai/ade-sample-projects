## Origin

`source/labsmart-cbc-sample.pdf` is a **Complete Blood Count (CBC) report** generated by
LabSmart's laboratory information system on its "Labsmart Software Sample Letterhead".
It is **2 pages, portrait**, a native PDF with a text layer: a patient and sample header
with a barcode and a QR code, a 13-row haematology results table with abnormal values
flagged `L` or `H` and set in bold, a clinical-notes panel, and two signature blocks.
Page 2 repeats the header and closes the report.

LabSmart links this PDF from its CBC report format page as an example of what its
software prints. It is a vendor specimen: the lab's phone, email and website are
placeholders (`+91 12345 67890`, `yourlabname@gmail.com`, `yourlabname.in`), the
registration number is printed masked, both signatures carry a "SAMPLE" stamp, and the
footer reads "NOT VALID FOR MEDICO LEGAL PURPOSE". The patient name is part of the
vendor's sample. The file is used unaltered.

**The shared drive's file was not used.** The ADE Documents drive held a different
document under this request: a branded diagnostics-lab report with a patient's name,
mobile number, visit ID and barcode, nothing marking it as a sample, and no source. It
appeared to be a real patient's report, so the operator rejected it, and it was neither
parsed nor copied into this repo. Full provenance is in `manifest.json` under
`source.origin`.

## Why this document

### Who processes it

A lab report carries a patient's test results against reference ranges. The results that
matter most are the ones flagged outside their range.

- **Hospitals and clinics:** results from outside labs are entered into the patient's
  electronic health record.
- **Health insurers and claims processors:** they check results to support claims and
  prior authorizations.
- **Life and health insurance underwriting:** underwriters review blood work submitted
  with an application.
- **Clinical research:** trial teams transcribe lab values from site reports into study
  databases.
- **Telehealth and personal health apps:** services read uploaded lab reports to track a
  patient's results over time.

### What it contains

- **A results table** with test name, result, unit and reference range in separate
  columns.
- **Abnormal flags in their own column,** `L` or `H`, with the whole row set in bold.
- **Reference ranges as text,** such as `13 - 17`, `4,800 - 10,800` and `< 2`.
- **A nested sub-panel:** the differential leucocyte count is an indented group under a
  header row with no value of its own.
- **Regional units,** such as platelets in `lakhs/cumm` (1 lakh = 100,000).
- **A barcode, a QR code** and two signature blocks with "SAMPLE" stamps.
