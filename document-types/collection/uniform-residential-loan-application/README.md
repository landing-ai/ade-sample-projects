# Uniform Residential Loan Application

Source assets for `landing.ai/document-type/uniform-residential-loan-application`.

## Sample document

`source/uniform-residential-loan-application.pdf` — a completed, e-signed Uniform
Residential Loan Application on the Fannie Mae Form 1003 / Freddie Mac Form 65 template
(effective 1/2021). The lender produced it in DocuTech. **8 pages, portrait (letter), with a text
layer**:

- Pages 1–6: the borrower sections. Personal information and employment income; assets and
  liabilities; real estate owned and the loan and property; declarations;
  acknowledgments, signature and military service; demographics and the loan originator.
- Pages 7–8: the lender's loan information (L1–L4), including the proposed monthly
  payment and the funds-to-close calculation.

The loan is a single-borrower, cash-out refinance of a primary residence on a 30-year
adjustable-rate (10/6) conventional loan.

### Clearance

Scribd is a user-upload site, not a publisher, so this was treated as a personal record:
a real borrower's application. Scribd blocks scripted downloads, so the operator
downloaded the file by hand. The source of record is the ID-only Scribd URL above.

**Before redaction, the file had not been edited since it was signed.** Its DocuTech
e-sign certification signature covered the whole file, and the SHA-512 digest of the
signed bytes matched the one inside the signature. The arithmetic is consistent across
the form:

- The five liabilities sum to line L4-E.
- Line H is the sum of D through G.
- The cash-back figure equals H minus K.
- The proposed payment total adds up.
- The P&I matches the loan amount, rate and term.

Every personal value was replaced **before** the document was parsed, so none of it
reached ADE. The operator then reviewed the redacted pages before the run.

- **Borrower**: the name, on the page 1 field, the title holder in L2 and the
  "Borrower Name:" footer on every page, became *Jane Q Sample*. The SSN became a
  reserved 987-65-432x number. The date of birth, the dependents' ages, the phones (now
  555-01xx) and the email (example.com) were replaced.
- **Addresses**: the current address and the subject property are the same property.
  Its street, city, ZIP and county became *1000 Main St*, *Anytown*, a fictitious ZIP and
  *Sample*.
- **Employment**: the employer name, address and phone became *Acme Hotel*,
  *PO Box 100, Hometown* and a 555-01xx number. The position title, start date and years
  in the line of work were replaced too.
- **Account numbers**: the liability and mortgage account numbers became random digits
  of the same shape, with their masking kept. Creditor names were kept.
- **Loan identifiers**: the lender loan number in every page header was replaced. The
  Universal Loan Identifier was rebuilt whole: a new random LEI with valid check digits,
  the new loan number, and recomputed ULI check digits.
- **Loan originator**: the individual originator's name, typed signature, NMLSR ID,
  state licence, email and phone were replaced. The originating company, its office
  address and its company IDs were kept.
- **Signatures**: the borrower's e-signature image, which carried an e-sign ID, was
  deleted and redrawn as *Jane Q Sample* in Indie Flower (SIL OFL). The originator's
  typed signature was replaced in the same font.
- **Barcodes**: the footer barcode image on each of the 8 pages was removed.
- **Demographics (page 6)**: every ethnicity, sex and race box was cleared, and
  **"I do not wish to provide this information" is ticked in each group**. This was done
  by swapping the form's own checked and unchecked checkbox drawings, so the page looks
  native. **The original selections are not represented anywhere in this folder.**
- **File structure**: the bookmark outline (its title held the borrower's name), the
  named destinations, the e-sign signature field with its certificate, and the document
  metadata were all removed.

Values were replaced with PyMuPDF redaction annotations, which remove the underlying
text, and stand-ins were written in the original font and size. The output was verified
in two ways: by re-extracting the text, and by scanning every decompressed object and
stream for the original values. The redaction rules live outside this repo.

**Residual risk.** The loan amount, the property value, the lender's office and the
signing dates are kept, because they are what the page demonstrates. Together they could
in principle be matched against a public county mortgage record. Nothing in this folder
names the borrower, the property or the city.

## Why this document

The collection's **application-stage mortgage document**: the form every US residential
mortgage starts from. It sits ahead of the rate lock, appraisal and closing disclosure
already here.

- **A government-standard form, filled by a loan origination system.** Fixed sections,
  fill-in lines, and values in Courier over a proportional form face.
- **Radio buttons and checkboxes carry most of the meaning.** Loan purpose, occupancy,
  marital status, citizenship, the declarations and refinance type are all selections,
  not text.
- **Arithmetic that crosses pages.** The page 2 liabilities feed L4 on page 8, and the
  page 3 mortgage balance feeds line D.

## Featured fields: the loan being applied for

All on page 3, section 4a. The request named no fields. Its notes, and the operator,
asked for impersonal ones only.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Loan amount | $404,500 |
| Loan purpose | Refinance |
| Property value | $608,200 |
| Occupancy | Primary Residence |
| Number of units | 1 |

Together they say what is being borrowed, why, against what, and for what use. Loan
amount is pinned to its 4a occurrence; it also appears three times on page 8. Property
value is pinned to its 4a occurrence rather than the 3a real-estate row. Gross monthly
income (base and total) grounds cleanly too, but it is on page 1, and an overlay shows
one page.

## What it surfaced

**Radio buttons read correctly.** Loan purpose, occupancy, citizenship, marital status,
refinance type, amortization type, and the declarations all extract as the selected
option. The demographic groups come back as "I do not wish to provide this information",
as the redacted page shows.

**Grounding is line-level on this form.** Loan amount and loan purpose sit on one form
line and share one box. Number of units and property value share another. Each crop
contains its value, but the overlay shows three boxes for five fields.

**Four checked boxes were parsed as unchecked.** In the 2c liabilities table, every
"To be paid off at or before closing" box is checked on the page. The parse reads only
the last row's box as checked and the other four as `[ ]`, so `to_be_paid_off` comes
back false for four of the five liabilities. The same kind of box in the 3a mortgage row
reads correctly.

**The ULI wraps and is truncated.** The Universal Loan Identifier prints across two
lines. The parse puts its second line after the field label, and extraction returns only
the first line, which is the LEI.

**Blank income lines came back as 0, not null.** Overtime, bonus and similar lines are
empty on the page but extract as 0, and their grounding points at the bare `$` label.

**Wrapped cells were joined correctly.** An account number that wraps onto a second line
in the liabilities table was joined into one value.

## Regenerating

The source PDF is the redacted file committed here. The original cannot be re-fetched by
script, and it must not be.

```bash
.venv/bin/python document-types/scripts/run_ade.py uniform-residential-loan-application                # ~22.30 credits
.venv/bin/python document-types/scripts/run_ade.py uniform-residential-loan-application --extract-only # schema iteration
.venv/bin/python document-types/scripts/build_images.py uniform-residential-loan-application           # free
.venv/bin/python document-types/scripts/inspect_fields.py uniform-residential-loan-application --page 3
```
