## Origin

`source/uniform-residential-loan-application.pdf` is a completed, e-signed **Uniform
Residential Loan Application** on the Fannie Mae Form 1003 / Freddie Mac Form 65 template
(effective 1/2021). The lender produced it in DocuTech. It is **8 pages, portrait (US
Letter)**, with a text layer:

- Pages 1–6: the borrower sections. Personal information and employment income; assets
  and liabilities; real estate owned and the loan and property; declarations;
  acknowledgments, signature and military service; demographics and the loan
  originator.
- Pages 7–8: the lender's loan information (L1–L4), including the proposed monthly
  payment and the funds-to-close calculation.

The loan is a single-borrower, cash-out refinance of a primary residence on a 30-year
adjustable-rate (10/6) conventional loan.

Scribd is a user-upload site, not a publisher, so this was treated as a personal record:
a real borrower's application. Scribd blocks scripted downloads, so the operator
downloaded the file by hand. The source of record is the ID-only Scribd URL above.

Before redaction, the file had not been edited since it was signed. Its DocuTech e-sign
certification signature covered the whole file, and the SHA-512 digest of the signed
bytes matched the one inside the signature. The arithmetic is consistent across the
form: the five liabilities sum to line L4-E, line H is the sum of D through G, the
cash-back figure equals H minus K, the proposed payment total adds up, and the P&I
matches the loan amount, rate and term.

Every personal value was replaced before the document was parsed, and the operator
reviewed the redacted pages first.

- **Borrower:** the name, on page 1, in L2 and in the "Borrower Name:" footer on every
  page, became *Jane Q Sample*. The SSN became a reserved 987-65-432x number. The date of
  birth, the dependents' ages, the phones (now 555-01xx) and the email (example.com) were
  replaced.
- **Addresses:** the current address and the subject property are the same property.
  Its street, city, ZIP and county became *1000 Main St*, *Anytown*, a fictitious ZIP and
  *Sample*.
- **Employment:** the employer name, address and phone became *Acme Hotel*, *PO Box 100,
  Hometown* and a 555-01xx number. The position title, start date and years in the line
  of work were replaced too.
- **Account numbers:** the liability and mortgage account numbers became random digits
  of the same shape, with their masking kept. Creditor names were kept.
- **Loan identifiers:** the lender loan number in every page header was replaced. The
  Universal Loan Identifier was rebuilt whole, with a new random LEI, the new loan
  number and valid check digits.
- **Loan originator:** the individual originator's name, typed signature, NMLSR ID,
  state license, email and phone were replaced. The originating company, its office
  address and its company IDs were kept.
- **Signatures:** the borrower's e-signature image, which carried an e-sign ID, was
  deleted and redrawn as *Jane Q Sample* in Indie Flower (SIL OFL). The originator's
  typed signature was replaced in the same font.
- **Barcodes:** the footer barcode image on each of the 8 pages was removed.
- **Demographics (page 6):** every ethnicity, sex and race box was cleared, and "I do
  not wish to provide this information" is ticked in each group, using the form's own
  checkbox drawings. **The original selections are not represented anywhere in this
  folder.**
- **File structure:** the bookmark outline (its title held the borrower's name), the
  named destinations, the e-sign signature field with its certificate, and the document
  metadata were all removed.

The redaction removed the underlying text, and the stand-ins were written in the
original font and size. The output was verified by re-extracting the text and by
scanning every decompressed object and stream for the original values. The redaction
rules live outside this repo.

**Residual risk.** The loan amount, the property value, the lender's office and the
signing dates are kept, because they are what the page demonstrates. Together they could
in principle be matched against a public county mortgage record. Nothing in this folder
names the borrower, the property or the city. Full provenance is in `manifest.json`
under `source.origin`.

## Why this document

### Who processes it

The Form 1003 is the application every US residential mortgage starts from, and the
data on it follows the loan through underwriting, closing and sale.

- **Mortgage lenders and brokers:** loan officers and processors take the application
  and check it against pay stubs, bank statements and credit reports.
- **Underwriting:** underwriters read income, assets, liabilities and declarations to
  decide on the loan.
- **Secondary market investors:** Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac and loan buyers check that the
  final application matches the loan data delivered.
- **Mortgage quality control and audit:** reviewers re-verify applications and check
  disclosures and demographic reporting for fair-lending compliance.
- **Loan servicing:** servicers board new loans with the borrower and property data
  from the application.

### What it contains

- **A government-standard form filled by a loan origination system,** with values in
  Courier over a proportional form face.
- **Radio buttons and checkboxes** for loan purpose, occupancy, marital status,
  citizenship, the declarations, refinance type and demographics.
- **A liabilities table** with a paid-off checkbox on every row and account numbers that
  wrap onto a second line.
- **Arithmetic across pages:** the page 2 liabilities feed L4 on page 8, and the page 3
  mortgage balance feeds line D.
- **The same value printed in several places,** such as the loan amount on page 3 and
  three times on page 8.
- **A loan identifier that wraps** across two lines.
- **A handwriting-style e-signature** and a typed originator signature.
