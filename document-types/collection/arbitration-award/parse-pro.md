**CASE SUMMARY**
In the Statement of Claim, Claimant asserted the following causes of action: violation of FINRA
Rule 2010; violation of FINRA Rule 3110; violation of FINRA Rule 4511; negligence; fraudulent
misrepresentation; negligent misrepresentation; tortious interference with expectation of
inheritance; and equitable indemnity. The causes of action relate to an account that was
allegedly intended to be transferred to Claimant after the death of the decedent account holder.


Unless specifically admitted in the Statement of Answer, Respondents denied the allegations
made in the Statement of Claim and asserted various affirmative defenses.


**RELIEF REQUESTED**
In the Statement of Claim, Claimant requested: damages in an unspecified amount based upon
the amount of the investment account of the time of surrender, plus interest that would have
accrued if the account were properly managed; damages in an amount equal to reasonable
attorneys' fees and costs incurred by Claimant in defending an underlying action; interest
assessed at the legal rate, if any, then prevailing in the state where the award is rendered, or at
the rate set by the Arbitrators; punitive damages; and such other and additional relief deemed
just and proper including but not limited to pre-award interest, attorneys' fees and arbitration
costs.


In the Statement of Answer, Respondents requested: dismissal of the Statement of Claim in all
respects; and that all forum fees in connection with this proceeding be assessed against
Claimant.


**OTHER ISSUES CONSIDERED AND DECIDED**
The Arbitrators acknowledge that they have each read the pleadings and other materials filed by
the parties.


AWARD
After considering the pleadings, the testimony and evidence presented at the hearing, and any
post-hearing submissions, the Panel has decided in full and final resolution of the issues
submitted for determination as follows:
 1. Respondents Janney Montgomery Scott LLC, Steven Alfred Pitassi, and Richard Anthony
Ranone are jointly and severally liable for and shall pay to Claimant the sum of $642,000.00
in compensatory damages.
 2. Claimant’s claims against Respondent Kristen Laura Verdeaux are denied.
 3. Any and all claims for relief not specifically addressed herein, including any requests for
punitive damages and attorneys’ fees, are denied.
