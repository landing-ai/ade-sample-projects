No. 165

Personally appeared before me, the undersigned, JUDGE OF THE PROBATE COURT, within
and for the County of Hamilton [William H Taft], who being
duly sworn, deposeth and saith, that he is more than twenty-one years of age, and has no lawful
wife living. And that [Helen L Herron]
is more than eighteen years of age, and has no lawful husband living, that she is a resident of
the County of Hamilton aforesaid; and the said [William H Taft]
further says, that said [Helen L Herron]
and affiant are not of nearer relation to each other than that of second cousin, and that
he knows of no legal objection to the Marriage contemplated between them. And further
this deponent saith not.


[SIGNED]
Sworn to and subscribed before me
this 19 day of June 1886
Herman Hoebel
PROBATE JUDGE.
By L Hauser Deputy Clerk.
Wm J Taft


MARRIAGE RETURN.


MARRIED, on the 19 day of June A.D. 1886
William H. Taft and Helen L. Herron
by me a Minister of the Gospel
A. Hogg
THE STATE OF OHIO
Minister of the Gospel
