Note that the first question is whether the change is being made with the intent to significantly improve the safety or effectiveness of the device, for example, to significantly improve clinical outcomes, to mitigate a known risk, in response to adverse events, etc. (Figure 1 – Main Flowchart). If so, the change likely could significantly affect safety or effectiveness and submission of a new 510(k) is likely required. If not, you should continue to follow the logic scheme shown in Figure 1, below.
