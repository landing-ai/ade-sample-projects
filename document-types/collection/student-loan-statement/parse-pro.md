# Other useful information

## Your loan balance

Your loan balance is based on the details we held
when we issued this statement. Your balance can
change if, for example:
 - your employer changes any repayments
they deducted from your wages
 - you travel overseas which may affect your
eligibility for an interest free loan
 - we receive more information from
StudyLink
 - an administration fee is charged.


**Interest-free loans**
If you've been living in New Zealand for 183 or
more consecutive days, you're eligible for an
interest free loan.


If you're not living in New Zealand or have been
out of New Zealand for 184 days or more, you
won't be eligible for an interest free loan, unless
you're being treated as overseas based - interest
free.


# How loan interest is calculated

If your loan is not interest free, the current annual
interest rate is 2.9%. Interest is calculated daily
using this rate. Interest may be adjusted when
transactions made in a previous statement period
haven't been shown on your account.


If you believe the interest we've charged is
incorrect then you can dispute this, but you must
do this within four months of the date of this
statement.


# Paying off your loan in full

If you are paying interest on your loan and want
to repay your loan in full, you have 30 days from
the date of this statement to pay the current loan
balance before further interest will be added.


**If you don't make loan payments on time**
If you do not pay on time we may charge you late
payment interest on the outstanding amount each
month, until you pay the overdue amount
(including late payment interest) in full.


The current annual late payment interest rate is
6.9%. This is reduced to 4.9% for amounts under
instalment arrangement.


# Having trouble paying?

There are a number of options available in myIR
to help you manage your repayments:
- If you're earning salary or wages, you can
apply for a reduced deduction rate
- You can apply for relief if you cannot afford
your payments
- You can request an instalment arrangement
for amounts you owe
- If you're going overseas you can apply for a
temporary repayment suspension.
