**DESCRIPTION OF OPERATIONS / LOCATIONS / VEHICLES (ACORD 101, Additional Remarks Schedule, may be attached if more space is required)**
Coverage applies to current ABCA member coaches in good standing while conducting direct one on one or group baseball or softball coaching, or
instruction, based on widely accepted industry best practices, at a practice or playing facility. Coverage is NOT extended to the following operations: facility
ownership, long-term lease arrangements, or 24-hour management responsibilities. Coverage is NOT extended to the following entities: teams, leagues,
camps, clinics, business entities, and respective officers, directors, and staff. Coverage will NOT extend to incidents that occurred prior to a coach joining the
ABCA, during any lapse in membership, or arising from non-baseball/softball related coaching activities. The certificate holder is added as an additional
insured, but only for liability caused, in whole or in part, by the acts or omissions of the named insured.
